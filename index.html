Welcome & Introduction to the Circle

The Golden Chain (Silsila) & Ādāb al-Dhikr

Welcome to Newcomers

Welcome to this gathering of light, knowledge, and spiritual purification. You are sitting in a circle dedicated to the remembrance of Allah and the study of His Prophet’s ﷺ words. This gathering is rooted in The Shadhili Way, one of the most respected and foundational paths of spiritual development (Tasawwuf) in the Islamic world. The ultimate goal of this path is to polish the mirror of the heart, allowing it to fully absorb the light of faith (Iman) and spiritual excellence (Ihsan).

The Golden Chain (Silsila) & Spiritual Lineage

In our tradition, spiritual guidance is not taken from books alone; it is passed from heart to heart through an unbroken, living chain of authorized teachers stretching back over a thousand years. This is known as the Golden Chain. This circle is established under the direct authorization and spiritual supervision of His Eminence, Shaykh Muhammad al-Yaqoubi (may Allah preserve him).

Shaykh Muhammad al-Yaqoubi is a world-renowned jurist and master of Hadith who traces his lineage directly back to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. He inherited the leadership of this spiritual path through his father, the great scholar Shaykh Ibrahim al-Yaqoubi, through a lineage of elite masters connecting directly to Imam Abul Hasan al-Shadhili (the founder of the path), and ultimately to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Etiquette of the Circle (Ādāb al-Dhikr)

  • 1.
    Pure Intention (Ikhlāṣ): Enter the circle solely seeking the pleasure of Allah ﷻ, affection for His Messenger ﷺ, and purification of the heart.
  • 2.
    Presence of Heart (Huḍūr): Sit in ritual purity (Wuḍū'), face the Qiblah where possible, and turn your inner focus entirely away from worldly distractions.
  • 3.
    Vocal Harmony (Ittihād al-Ṣawt): Maintain a gentle, measured, and rhythmic collective speed. Blend your voice seamlessly with the Muqaddam without overriding the circle.
"Whosoever remembers Allah in a gathering, Allah remembers them in a gathering far superior."
